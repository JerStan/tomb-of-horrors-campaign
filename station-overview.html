The preparation screen is available. You can save an investigator using your existing Traveller character or the starting captain shown in the public preview.

Open private campaign setup

Owner sign-in is required. Your investigator is saved privately on the laptop, separately from Tomb of Horrors.

The published mystery

The original adventure is Murder on Arcturus Station, Classic Traveller Adventure 11. A corporate executive is murdered on Station Three in the Arcturus Belt, and the referee chooses the guilty party from nine suspects.

Publisher’s original adventure reference

This setup uses the original edition. The later Mysteries on Arcturus Station is a reimagining.

What remains before play

The published suspect backgrounds, motives, methods, alibis, clues and station maps still need to be added from the adventure copy. The public preview does not include that complete material.

Investigation play has not started. The referee’s hidden solution will be stored privately and fixed before the first move, so the culprit and evidence cannot change to fit your answers.
