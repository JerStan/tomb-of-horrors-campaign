Begin your investigation

Prepare your investigator from an existing Classic Traveller character or choose the published captain and companions. Investigate through searches, interviews, records and forensic comparisons.

Open private investigation

Owner sign-in is required. Your investigator and case are saved privately on the laptop, separately from Tomb of Horrors. The laptop must remain online.

The published mystery

The original adventure is Murder on Arcturus Station, Classic Traveller Adventure 11. A corporate executive is murdered on Station Three in the Arcturus Belt, and the referee chooses the guilty party from nine suspects.

Publisher’s original adventure reference

This setup uses the original edition. The later Mysteries on Arcturus Station is a reimagining.

Your own fixed mystery

Each new mystery chooses a different murderer from the nine published suspects and fixes the motive, method, timeline and evidence before play. Copying current progress keeps that same case. A beginning branch can replay it or choose a new mystery.

Published station plans, zoomable pictures, clues, character details, checkpoints, backups, setting-aware music and event effects use the shared GameVault interface. The hidden solution and original source material stay private.

Rules available in this release

This release uses the investigation procedures in the original Adventure 11. Full Classic Traveller combat, career generation and other core-rule procedures remain pending. Optional stress detection and truth drugs are not yet fully automated.
